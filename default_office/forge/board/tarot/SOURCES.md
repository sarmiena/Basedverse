## The meanings (`meanings.json`)

Each card's meaning, as the room shows it when you pick a card out of a reading: A. E. Waite's own, from *The Pictorial
Key to the Tarot* (1910), the book written to go with this deck, in the text of its 1918 reprint by L. W. De Laurence (*The
Illustrated Key to the Tarot*, Project Gutenberg eBook #43548, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43548): public domain.
For each card, what Waite says of it (the first of what he writes on a major's picture, or his description of a minor's),
and its divinatory meanings upright and reversed. Waite gives the Two of Cups no reversed meaning. `meanings.py` made it
from the Gutenberg text: `python3 meanings.py pg43548.txt meanings.json`.
